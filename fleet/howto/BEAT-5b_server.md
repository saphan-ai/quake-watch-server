# BEAT 5b · quakewatch-server · You decide

- what this step is: the owner reads the work and the review, and decides
- for QuakeWatch: 21–22 October 2026, the first wagon

## What you do

In the web console you see the wagon: what was built, the tests, the master's check and the review. You choose one of three:

- **accept**: the work goes into the finished code;
- **accept with conditions**: it goes in, and the reviewer's remaining points become written tasks for later wagons;
- **reject**: it goes back for another round, with the reviewer's words attached.

For the first wagon the owner rejected. The second round fixed the tests so they could fail; the second review said "accept with
conditions"; the owner accepted, and the code went into the finished version. One click recorded all three decisions together:
the wagon is done, the review's verdict, and the merge.

## Why

The agents advise; a person decides. Nothing goes into the finished code, and nothing is published, without the owner.
